What the comparison measures

The surveys are 24 hours and 17 minutes apart. An interpreter drew the observed boundaries from aerial thermal imagery. The scenario compares buffered area with the second observed shape, using the union for overall overlap and the difference between surveys for new area. Mapped area and the published flight acreage can differ slightly.

The fixed-distance scenario uses no wind, fuel or terrain inputs. It illustrates the trade-off between missing new area and including area outside the observed perimeter on this incident.
