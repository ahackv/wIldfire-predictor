Data and method

Fire reports and weather

The model scores 96 Northern California cells using past days with similar weather and season. The outcome is a new wildfire report anywhere in the selected cell the following day.

The starter covers 314,880 cell-days with gridMET weather and USDA fire discovery records. The replay shows the held-out 2023–24 dates.
