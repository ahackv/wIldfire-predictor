    Compute benchmark

    The weather model ranks each cell by comparing its recent weather with earlier days in that cell. The location-and-month baseline uses that month’s historical report rate.

    The squared distance is the squared query norm plus the squared history norm minus twice their dot product. Matrix multiplication computes the dot products for tiles of 16 forecast days across independent cells.

    Weather inputs stop two days before the target date. Outcome labels use the recorded discovery date.

    Fire reports and weather

    The model scores 96 Northern California cells using past days with similar weather and season. The outcome is a new wildfire report anywhere in the selected cell the following day.

    The starter covers 314,880 cell-days with gridMET weather and USDA fire discovery records. Typical weather comes from 2016–20 for the same cell and month.
